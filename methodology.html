General Methodology

The Knowledge Gap

We analysed our two monuments through ArCo and Wikidata ontologies to identify relevant classes and properties by leveraging from different sparql queries we ran into ArCo and Wikidata query services,then we compared our results with our external valuable ressources to identify whether there are gaps or not in our two knowledge graph websites.

Tools Used

After obtaining the result we applied the different LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Deepseek) through multiple prompting techniques by giving the following sparql keywords to be included to our project building process: OPTIONAL, DISTINCT, UNION, FILTER and REGEX, LIMIT, ORDER BY . Moreover, these LLMs were helpful to the Creation of new RDF triples via CONSTRUCT queries based on LLM-generated data and their semantic validation. Finally, we end up with a Development of a navigable website using GitHub..
