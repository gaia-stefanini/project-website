LLM Comparative Behavior

Initially, ChatGPT was helpful in setting the SPARQL queries, but then we faced some issues, such as interferences when we made prompts, probably due to the influence of its internal memory, which kept our previous chats and influenced the answers we received. To manage this problem, we tried DeepSeek and Gemini and found better outcomes for our project. Prompting techniques were also very important for obtaining accurate responses when we employed them with different LLMs..
