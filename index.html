BOLOGNA'S CHURCHES:SAN PETRONIO AND SAND DOMENICO

A Knowledge Graph Approach to Historical Religious Monuments in Bologna.

Abstract

The aim of this project is to investigate two of the most important churches located in Bologna: the Basilica di San Petronio and the Basilica di San Domenico. The project centres on enriching the ArCo and Wikidata Knowledge Graphs by creating new sets of RDF triples related to these two churches.. Remember that whenever you mention a web-browsable concept like Wikidata or SPARQL, you must include a link!
